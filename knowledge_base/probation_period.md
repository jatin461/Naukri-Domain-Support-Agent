# Probation Period Policy

New hires serve a probation period of six months from the joining date. A review is held at the end of month three and again before month six, and the manager records whether performance targets are being met. Probation can be extended once, by up to three months, if a review shows a gap that can be closed. Employment is confirmed in writing, and until then either side may end the employment with 30 days of notice.
