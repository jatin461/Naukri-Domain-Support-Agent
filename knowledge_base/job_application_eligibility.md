# Job Application Eligibility

An applicant is eligible for a posted role when the resume shows the minimum years of experience stated in the job description and any mandatory qualification listed for that role. Applicants must be at least 18 years old and legally permitted to work in the country where the position is based. A candidate may hold at most three active applications with the same employer at one time, and a rejected candidate may reapply for the same role after 90 days. Applications without a current contact email stay at Applied until that field is completed.
