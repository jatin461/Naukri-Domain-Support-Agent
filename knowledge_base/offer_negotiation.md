# Offer Negotiation Policy

Offers are issued with a salary inside the approved band for the role and level. A candidate may make one counter-proposal within three working days of receiving the offer, and the hiring manager can approve a revision of up to 10 percent above the original figure without further sign-off. Anything higher needs written approval from the HR head. A revised offer is final, and any offer lapses seven days after issue if it is unsigned.
