# Interview Scheduling Process

When an application reaches Screening, a recruiter proposes at least two interview slots within the next five working days. The candidate confirms one slot through the portal, and the status changes to Interview Scheduled only after that confirmation. A candidate may reschedule once without penalty if the request is made at least 24 hours before the slot. Each interview round is limited to 60 minutes, and the panel must submit written feedback within two working days of the round.
