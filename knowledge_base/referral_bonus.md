# Referral Bonus Policy

An employee who refers an external candidate earns a referral bonus once the referred person joins and completes 90 days of employment. The bonus is INR 25,000 for non-managerial roles and INR 50,000 for managerial roles. A referral must be submitted through the portal before the candidate's first interview to qualify. Members of the hiring panel and the HR team cannot claim a bonus for roles they are hiring for.
